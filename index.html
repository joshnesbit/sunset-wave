hi, neighbor.a wave of 100 neighbor gatherings, one block at a time.

On March 20th, we're hosting 100+ small gatherings on our actual blocks, with the people we've lived next to for years. Coffee and donuts in a driveway. Chalk on the sidewalk. A pot of soup on the front steps. Nothing formal, nothing to permit. Just us, out front, getting a little bit closer.

host on your block

Tuck an invitation under a few doors, put something out, and see who shows up. Two hours is plenty. Every host gets $200 for whatever the block needs.

join a gathering

Find your block on the map. If a neighbor is already hosting, you're invited. Walk over on the day and say hi. If nobody is yet, lend a hand or take the block yourself.

  1. it starts super simple. An invitation tucked under doors and doormats. A folding table. Coffee.
  2. a few or a few dozen neighbors show up. Someone looks out the window and smiles at what's unfolding.
  3. we meet the people next door. We find that we care about each other, and about our shared place.
  4. on march 20th we look down our corridors and see a wave of gatherings, from the park to Sloat, the ocean to 19th.
