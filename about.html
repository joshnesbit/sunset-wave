About

What is Sunset Wave?

Sunset Wave is a wave of 100+ neighbor gatherings, of all creative forms, on our actual blocks with our actual neighbors. All on one day: Saturday, March 20, 2027, the spring equinox.

The idea is simple. We meet folks we've lived next to for years. We get to know about each other, and find that we care about each other as well as our shared place. We start the process of coming together and shaping the neighborhood of our dreams.

It starts super simple. An invitation tucked under doors and doormats. Coffee and donuts in a driveway. A few or a few dozen neighbors getting together. Someone looking out the window and smiling at what's unfolding. On March 20th, we'll look down our Sunset corridors and see a wave of gatherings. We'll gather, and our neighborhood will get a little bit closer.

How hosting works

  • Pick your spot. Your driveway, your front steps, the sidewalk, a corner of the park. Wherever people can see you from the street.
  • Pick your hour. Morning coffee, an afternoon grill, chalk and lemonade after nap time. All day means you choose.
  • Put something out. Donuts and a coffee urn is a real gathering. So is a pot of soup, or a table of art supplies, or a record player.
  • Invite the block. A printed invitation under every door. We'll give you one to print, plus a sidewalk sign.
  • Get $200. Every host gets $200, in hand at Host Night on Saturday, March 6 at 4114 Judah. Not a reimbursement, not an application. It's for whatever your block needs.
  • Afterward, send one photo and one line. That's the whole report, and together they become the record of the day.

No permits, no street closures, nothing formal. Rain or shine; an open garage is the classic Sunset gathering anyway. Anyone who lives in the Outer Sunset can host. Club membership is not required.

How joining works

You don't need to sign up to join. Find your block on the map. If a neighbor is already hosting, walk over on March 20th and say hi. That's the whole idea. If you'd rather not host but want to help, chairs, ice, a folding table, or an extra pair of hands, say so on your block's tile and we'll connect you with the host. And if nobody has claimed your block yet, that person could be you.

Who's behind this

Sunset Wave is a project of the Sunset Social Club, a neighborhood club in San Francisco's Sunset with a simple vision: everyone knows someone on every block. The club meets for West Side Wednesdays, with shared meals, games, and skill-sharing, and is open to residents and friends of the neighborhood. Club members and other neighbors are the first hosts, and a handful of avenue captains will be checking in on hosts along their stretch of avenues, from sign-up to the day itself.

Josh keeps the map. Say hi at Host Night, or find him on 48th between Irving and Judah on the day.

Where the idea comes from

We didn't invent this. Sunset Wave follows the microgrant gathering recipe: small grants, real gatherings, neighbors with neighbors. The pattern is simple enough to repeat anywhere, and it works because saying yes takes three minutes instead of an application.

  • With Neighbors A national effort supporting local microgrant programs that fund small neighborhood gatherings. "Small grants. Real gatherings. Neighbors with neighbors." Their guides, templates, and the stories of other neighborhoods running gathering waves are the closest thing this project has to a playbook.
  • Microgrant Gatherings (Connective Tissue and the Trust for Civic Life) The recipe we're running at 100 hosts times $200. Their Charlottesville pilot budgeted for 30 hosts, expected five applications, and got 70. Two lessons carried over: demand outruns supply when saying yes is easy, and reporting should be one photo and one sentence.
  • Outer Sunset Today The neighborhood's daily dashboard for events and community life. Once enough blocks have said yes, the day's gatherings will be listed there too.
  • The Relational Technology Project This site was built with Relational Builder, an open-source app builder for tools that strengthen neighborhood connection. The code is public, and any neighborhood is welcome to copy it for their own wave.

Questions

Do I need to be a Sunset Social Club member? No. Anyone who lives in the Outer Sunset can host or join.

What if someone on my block is already hosting? Great, walk over. Or offer to help them. Two gatherings on one block is also fine; the map just tracks one host per block so the wave spreads.

What if it rains? Rain or shine. An open garage is the classic Sunset gathering. A one-week slide to March 27 only for a real storm, called by the Thursday before.

What does the club do with my phone number? A captain uses it to reach you before Host Night and on the day. It's never shown on the map.

I want to help make the whole thing happen. We need avenue captains, translators for a Chinese and Spanish invitation, and folks to help at Host Night. Come to West Side Wednesday, or reach the club through sunsetsocialclub.org.
